About IBRI

Bharat ka Apna Battle Royale

IBRI (Indian Battle Royale Indus) is not just a game — it is Bharat's first sovereign gaming ecosystem, built for over 500 million Indian mobile gamers. Developed by XMONSTERGAMINGSTUDIOSOFFICIAL.

At its core, IBRI delivers a console-grade Battle Royale experience in just 350MB — running on networks as low as 10 KBPS and budget smartphones under ₹8,000.

The game features 25 Indian characters, 11 maps, 32 weapons, 5 vehicles, and a ₹5 Micro-Sachet model. What separates IBRI is its AI V10.1.5 Anti-Cheat — 3-Strike Protocol, Phantom-X, Annihilator Mode, Virtual Jail.

"जब एक भारतीय गेमर IBRI खेलता है, तो वह सिर्फ match नहीं जीतता — वह अपनी सभ्यता, पहचान, और आत्मनिर्भर भारत का गौरव जीतता है। Jeet Hamari, Pehchaan Hamari।"
